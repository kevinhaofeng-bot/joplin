## Design Context

### Users

Joplin Lite is a personal macOS notebook for one experienced Joplin user who migrated from Evernote years ago. The primary job is to capture, find, and edit notes without relearning a complex product. The user keeps large, long-lived notes and attachments, also uses official Joplin Android clients, and values visible data safety over decorative features.

### Brand Personality

Calm, direct, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a focused native writing tool: quiet while editing, clear when something needs attention, and honest about features that are not ready. It must never feel like a dashboard, a developer console, or an over-configurable knowledge-management system.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use restrained editorial minimalism with a familiar macOS desktop rhythm and an Evernote-like focus on the note itself. Prefer a warm, lightly tinted canvas, crisp hierarchy, thin structural separators, and Joplin blue only for meaningful selection or status. Use one readable system-aware sans-serif family, no web-font download, no gradients, glassmorphism, ornamental cards, oversized icons, or animated spectacle. Light appearance is the visual reference, with an equally restrained automatic dark appearance through `prefers-color-scheme`. Respect reduced-motion preferences and do not depend on color alone.

### Design Principles

1. Make writing and finding a note the only visually dominant jobs.
2. Show just one obvious next action; unavailable future features remain visible only when that prevents false expectations.
3. Use whitespace, alignment, and type before borders, shadows, containers, or color.
4. Keep status language short and concrete, especially for isolation, synchronization, and failure states.
5. Preserve accessible semantics, keyboard focus, readable contrast, and reduced motion even when simplifying.
